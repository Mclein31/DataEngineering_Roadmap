## ⚠️ Certification Rules & Cost Discipline
1. **Never Study for Certifications Before Building**: A certification without portfolio repositories is viewed with skepticism by engineering managers. Code first, certify second.
2. **Utilize 100% Free Preparation Materials**: Use official AWS Skill Builder and Databricks Academy free learning paths. Never pay for expensive commercial bootcamps.
3. **Practice Exam Benchmark**: Do not book an exam until you score $\ge 85\%$ on at least 3 timed practice exams (Tutorials Dojo).
