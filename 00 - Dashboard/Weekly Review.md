# 📊 Weekly Sprint Review

Execute this review every Sunday evening to audit progress, assess blockers, evaluate time allocation, and set deterministic sprint goals for the upcoming week.

---

## Weekly Sprint Cadence
- **Target Hours**: 10–15 hours/week
- **Distribution**: Weekdays (1–2 hrs/day), Saturday (3.5 hrs deep project block), Sunday (1.5 hrs retro)
